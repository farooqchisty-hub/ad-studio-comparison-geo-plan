FAQ

Arcads questions people ask.

Can I use Arcads AI for free?

Only for 72 hours. Arcads has no free plan; its trial gives 1,000 credits, enough for one talking-actor video, and requires a card. The $110 Starter plan begins automatically unless you cancel. Paid plans include 100 free standard images a day.

How much does Arcads AI cost per month?

Starter costs $110 a month for 8,000 credits, Creator $220 for 16,000 and Pro $550 for 48,000, per Arcads' Shopify listing on 6 Nov 2026. Yearly billing is 20% off. A talking-actor video uses 800 credits, about $11 on Starter.

What is the best free alternative to Arcads AI?

We make Brevo Ads, so read this with that in mind. Brevo Ads gives 3 free generations with no card, then costs $19 a month for static and UGC video ads. Arcads publishes a bigger actor library and can post ads for you.

Is Arcads AI safe?

FRESHR SAS, a French company, runs Arcads and says its actors are real people who consented and get paid. Check two terms first: the trial rolls into a paid plan, and article 13.3.2 can require you to delete videos if an actor withdraws.

Is Arcads worth it?

Yes, if talking-actor video is your main format and you make at least ten a month: Starter covers ten at $11 each. Trustpilot reviewers split: on 6 Nov 2026 it showed 2.6 from 189 reviews, 41% five-star and 49% one-star.

Method

Brevo Ads publishes this page and is one of the two products compared. Every Arcads fact comes from Arcads' own site, help center, terms or Shopify listing, read that day; we label third-party pages. Shopify showed Arcads prices in INR for our location; at the rate Shopify shows (1 INR = 0.0106 USD) they come to about $110, $220 and $551. All three divide evenly at ₹94.51 per dollar into $110, $220 and $550, the list prices Wireflow reports16. "Not described" means we did not find it on the pages checked; Arcads may still offer it. Google's AI Overview for "arcads pricing" said Arcads has no standard free trial15; Arcads' help center describes one512. We took the screenshots in a logged-out browser, then cropped and annotated them. They show no ad creative.

Cost per ad is monthly list price divided by ads per month. Arcads: Starter $110 / 8,000 credits, Creator $220 / 16,000, Pro $550 / 48,000; one Arcads 1.0 actor video (up to 2 minutes) is 800 credits, so $11.00, $11.00 and $9.17. Standard images are free up to 100 per rolling 24 hours on paid plans, then typically 8 to 32 credits ($0.11 to $0.44 on Starter). Brevo Ads: Studio Mini $19 (5+ videos, 50+ images), Studio $49 (10+, 100+), Studio Max $199 (20+, 200+) per the plan card, so $3.80, $4.90 and $9.95 per video and $0.38, $0.49 and $1.00 per image at most. Prices change; check each vendor before you buy.
