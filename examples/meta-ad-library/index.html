How to use the Meta Ad Library (2026)

The Meta Ad Library is Meta's free, public search of every active Facebook and Instagram ad. Anyone can use it without logging in.

Step by step

How do you search the Meta Ad Library?

Ten screens from a logged-out browser, with Nike as the example. We removed every ad creative.

  1. Pick a country and All ads.

    You only see ads delivered in that country. All ads covers brand research. The search box stays locked until you choose a category.

  2. Type the brand and choose its Page.

    Pressing Enter runs a keyword search. The Page under Advertisers lists only that brand's ads. Big brands run more than one Page, so check each.

  3. Read the results bar.

    Nike's US Page had about 95 active ads. Search inside one brand for words like "bundle." Filters and sorting sit here. Active ads is the default.

  4. Narrow it with five filters.

    Language drops ads that mix languages. Platform covers six Meta apps, Threads and WhatsApp included. Media type separates images, memes and videos. Inactive returns only EU, UK and political archives. Dates narrow the window.

  5. Sort by impressions.

    Sort by has two options. Impressions: high to low is the default and puts the most-seen ads first. Outside the EU and UK you get the order with no counts.

  6. Read the ad card.

    Started 20 Jun 2025: 504 days live on 6 Nov 2026, dated in Pacific Time. Icons show where the advertiser approved the ad to run. Multiple versions means Meta mixes the assets for each audience.

  7. Open See ad details.

    The card data repeats at the top. Page through the versions, six here. The advertiser's accounts sit on the right. The US view stops there and shows no reach or spend.

  8. Switch to an EU country for reach.

    Open the same ad with France selected. Tabs split EU and UK data. Targeted countries come first, then age and gender. Estimated EU reach: 1,063,111 accounts. Reach by country, age and gender.

  9. Check Page transparency.

    The About tab holds the Page's history. Created 21 May 2008, renamed once. NIKE, Inc. is responsible for it. The tab lists its managers' countries. That helps you spot renamed or recycled Pages.

  10. Use keyword search to scan a niche.

    "nike" as a keyword returned about 21,000 US ads, against 95 on Nike's Page. It matches ad text and Page names, so other advertisers appear. This card also reached the EU.

Winners

How do you spot a winning ad?

No single signal proves an ad makes money. Look for three that agree.

  • Days running

    A start date months back.

    Limit: pauses and profit stay hidden.

  • Versions

    Two or more versions under one ad.

    Limit: Meta builds these for dynamic ads.

  • Iterations

    New ads that reuse one hook or layout.

    Limit: copies may just be extra ad sets.

  • Same landing page

    Many ads pointing at one product.

    Limit: home-page links say little.

  • Top of the sort

    First under Impressions: high to low.

    Limit: order only, no counts.

API

Is there a Meta Ad Library API?

What it covers

Political ads from the past seven years. Any ad delivered in the EU or UK in the past year.

Who can use it

A Facebook account, ID confirmation and a Meta developer app.

Watch out

US-only commercial ads are missing. Meta's API page calls the EU figure "total impressions." The library calls it reach.

FAQ

Meta Ad Library questions.

Is the Meta Ad Library free?

Yes. Anyone can search facebook.com/ads/library without an account. You only need to log in to report an ad, view adult content or download search results.

Is the Facebook Ad Library the same as the Meta Ad Library?

Yes. Facebook Ad Library is the older name, and the tool still lives at facebook.com/ads/library. It also covers Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, Threads and Audience Network, so it doubles as the Instagram ad library.

Can you see how much a competitor spends on Meta ads?

Only for ads about social issues, elections or politics, which show spend ranges. Commercial ads never show spend. EU and UK ads show estimated reach instead, the closest free proxy.

How do you tell how long a Facebook ad has been running?

Read "Started running on" on the ad card and count the days to today. Meta records dates in Pacific Time and hides pauses, so treat the count as a maximum.

Why can't I find a competitor's ads?

Check the country first, because ads appear only in countries where Meta delivered them. Then try the brand's other Pages, creator partnership ads and a keyword search. A new ad can take up to 24 hours to appear.

Can you download or save ads from the library?

The library has no save button. Logged-in users can download search results after accepting Meta's terms. Scraping needs Meta's written permission, and the creative still belongs to the advertiser.
