The rule

What is the Instagram safe zone for ads?

It's the part of a Stories or Reels ad the app never covers. Meta's Ads Guide says to keep about 14% of the top, 35% of the bottom and 6% of each side free of text and logos. Its diagram also blocks a strip 40% tall and 21% wide in the corner where Reels shows the like and share buttons.

FAQ

Questions about Meta safe zones.

What is the safe zone for Instagram Reels ads?

Keep text and logos out of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% on each side of a 9:16 Reels ad. Meta's diagram also blocks a 40%-tall, 21%-wide area at the bottom right, behind the like and share buttons.

What are the safe zones for Instagram Stories in 2026?

Meta's Ads Guide gives Stories ads the same margins as Reels. On a 1080 × 1920 Story, keep 269 px clear at the top, 672 px at the bottom and 65 px on each side. Guides that quote 155 or 250 px are describing organic Stories.

What is the 4:5 safe zone for Instagram posts?

Meta publishes no pixel margins for 1:1 or 4:5 Feed ads. Its Help Center says to keep the bottom and side edges clear, so this checker draws a 6% line as a guide. A 9:16 video shown in Feed follows the Reels safe zone.

Is Facebook 4:5 or 9:16?

Both. Meta recommends 4:5 (1440 × 1800 px) for Facebook Feed and 9:16 (1440 × 2560 px on Instagram) for Stories and Reels. If you upload one size, Ads Manager pads, expands or crops it for other placements, so check those versions before you publish.

Did Meta change the safe zones in 2026?

Meta's help page, checked 6 Nov 2026, applies one 9:16 Safe Zone to Stories, Reels, Feed and Facebook in-stream reels. Some 2026 guides quote a 20% bottom margin or 8% sides. Meta's own Ads Guide says 35% and 6%, so this checker uses those.

How the check works

Your browser reads the file with FileReader and draws it on a canvas, so nothing leaves your device. The checker scales the image so its long side is 384 px, measures edge contrast in an 8 px grid, and flags cells whose contrast runs well above the image's own median. Text and logos make dense, sharp edges, so they trip it. It can't read words, so treat a flag as a place to look. The checker flags a zone when at least 2 cells land in it.

For images that aren't 9:16, the checker assumes Ads Manager pads them to 9:16. Ads Manager may also expand or crop them. Feed previews show the image at 1:1 to 4:5 and crop taller images from the center. Meta gives no Feed margin, so the dashed line marks 6% as a guide. For video, the checker reads one frame at a time. The app buttons in the frames are a guide, and their exact spots vary by device.
