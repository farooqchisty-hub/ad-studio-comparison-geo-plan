FAQ

What else do people ask about skincare ads?

What are some effective skincare product ads?

The ones brands keep running. In our 6 Nov 2026 sample of 247 live skincare ads on Meta, bundles (81%), problem-first opening lines (79%) and video (77%) were the likeliest to have run 90+ days.

How long should a Facebook ad run?

Give a new ad at least a week before you judge it. Meta says delivery stabilizes after about 50 optimization events in 7 days. Ads that keep working can run for months: the median in our skincare sample was 139 days.

Which Facebook ads are most successful?

You can’t see other brands’ results, because the Ad Library shows no sales and showed no spend for any ad we pulled. Days running is the closest public signal. Use it to pick ideas to test, then judge your version on your own numbers.

How do I advertise a skincare product on Facebook?

Start from a structure that lasts: a problem-first opening line, proof, a bundle, mid-length copy and a Shop now button. Build it around your product in Brevo Ads, then download it and upload it to Meta.

How we built the sample

On 6 Nov 2026 we pulled 11 keyword searches of active ads from Meta’s Ad Library through a third-party collector: nine terms in the US (skincare, serum, moisturizer, sunscreen, acne, retinol, cleanser, niacinamide, hyperpigmentation, with skincare pulled twice) and skincare in Ireland. 996 rows gave 810 unique ads. We kept 247 ads from 103 skincare brands that link to their own store, after dropping retailers, marketplaces, clinics, apps, devices, makeup, haircare, supplements, ads without a landing link and duplicate creatives.

How we classified ads

  • Days running: 6 Nov 2026 minus the Library’s “Started running on” date.
  • Format: Meta’s display format. Dynamic creative rotates a set of versions inside one ad.
  • Hook: the opening line, sorted by an ordered keyword rubric (first match wins). Offers: keyword flags on the full copy. Free gifts (7 ads) and free shipping (8) were too rare to chart.
  • Copy length: word count of the primary text.

Limits

  • The Library shows only live ads. Ads that stopped early drop out, which pushes the 90-day share up.
  • Keyword search ranks by relevance and favors older ads. sprites.ai counted every live creative from 13 brands on 20 Sep 2026 and found a 21-day median against our 139.
  • The Library gives a start date but no daily delivery, and it showed no spend for any of the 810 ads.
  • We hatch any group under 30 ads, so treat those as directional. The under-30-day group has 25 ads.
  • The rubric misreads some lines: a product launch that names a skin concern counts as problem-first.
  • Capping each brand at 3 ads (170 ads) kept every direction reported here.

Legal note

Meta’s terms require its written permission for automated data collection. This prototype used a third-party collector.
